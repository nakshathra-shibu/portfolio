About me

The mind behind the circuits.

I'm an Electronics and Communication Engineering student at SCTCE, curious about how technology works and how it can make a real difference in people's lives.

What pulls me towards IoT is that it makes the physical world talk. A tiny chip, a sensor and a Wi-Fi signal can let a plant say it's thirsty, a room adjust itself, or a wearable watch over someone's health. I'm excited by that moment when a simple circuit becomes something that quietly helps a person.

Right now I'm building my foundation one step at a time: Python, electronics fundamentals and web development, with Arduino and ESP32 as my next steps.
